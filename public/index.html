01 The building

The best seat in the house.

Twenty-nine residential floors, stacked so that nearly every residence takes a western or northern exposure — the skyline, the Queensboro Bridge, the ferries working the East River.

The Larkin stands at the corner of 46th Avenue and 5th Street in Hunters Point. The East River is one block west. The 7 at Vernon Blvd–Jackson Ave is two blocks east, and Grand Central is one stop from there, about nine minutes.

Halbrook Property Group completed the building in 2023. Faircloth Rossi drew it, Studio Marlowe did the interiors, and Bower & Field planted the fourth floor. Twenty-six thousand square feet of amenities sit on three levels, and all three face the same way you do.

The Larkin does not sell escape from the city. It sells the best seat in the house.

02 Residences

Ten plans. One direction.

Floors 5 through 27 hold the standard stack, lines A through L, eleven residences to a typical floor. Ceilings are 9 feet 4 inches, floors are 7-inch white oak, and every residence has a Bosch washer and a vented dryer.

Floors 28 through 33 are the West Collection — 28 residences above the setback. Ceilings at 10 feet 6 inches, quartzite waterfall islands, Fisher & Paykel refrigeration, and private terraces on the 28th-floor setback. West and north exposures only.

04 Amenities

Three floors, one direction.

Twenty-six thousand square feet on three levels — The Works on three, The Terrace and its 62-foot pool on four, The Overlook on thirty-four. No amenity fee. Access is included in rent.

03 Third floor

The Works

Four thousand two hundred square feet with Technogym strength and cardio, three Peloton bikes, and a 40-foot turf sprint lane along the west wall. Open five in the morning until midnight; your fob is the only key you need.

  • The Studio — yoga, mat pilates, and resident-led classes
  • The Recovery Suite — infrared sauna, cold plunge at 50 degrees, two treatment rooms
  • The Larder — demonstration kitchen and a 12-seat private dining room, reservable
  • The Study — co-working lounge, four phone rooms, two conference rooms
  • The Pet Spa — raised wash basin, hand sprayer, forced-air dryer
  • The Bike Room — 220 tagged spaces, one per residence, no charge

04 Fourth floor

The Terrace

The 62-foot pool, six cabanas, and a landscaped deck turned toward the skyline. The pool runs May 15 through September 30 and opens only when the lifeguard is on the chair — that is city code, not house policy. The deck stays open year-round.

  • Four gas grills, two firepits, and a pergola that seats twenty
  • The Green — 1,400 square feet of dog run, hosed down every morning at seven
  • The Playroom and a gated toddler yard off the deck
  • Laundry room on four, in addition to the washer and dryer in your residence

34 Thirty-fourth floor

The Overlook

Three thousand one hundred square feet at the top of the building, floor-to-ceiling glass on the west wall, and a sky terrace at 372 feet. A chef’s kitchen and a 14-seat table at one end, sofas at the other. Most nights it belongs to the whole building.

  • Screening room, eighteen seats in three tiers, 12-foot screen
  • The Reading Room — quiet lounge, no calls, no meetings, never reservable
  • West-facing sky terrace, open to every resident, closes at eleven
  • Private events Friday through Sunday evenings, sixty-five people, $500 refundable deposit

Service and everyday things

  • Attended lobby, 24 hours, and a live-in resident manager
  • Package room with Luxer lockers and cold storage
  • The Study, open around the clock with your fob
  • 84-space attended garage, EV charging, $425 a month
  • Private storage, $95 to $240 a month by locker size
  • Two pets per residence, 50 pounds each. No pet rent.
  • Smoke-free building, terraces included
  • No amenity fee. There isn’t one.
